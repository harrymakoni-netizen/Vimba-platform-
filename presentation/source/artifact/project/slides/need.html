(0:48–1:10 · 22 seconds) And the need is growing. The Voluntary Media Council of Zimbabwe received 39 complaints in 2024, up from 31. Accuracy and fairness were the largest share, and redress from online platforms was hard to get. Small newsrooms have no tool for this, so accountability gets enforced from outside. For media freedom, newsrooms should practise it themselves.
