Ready for your questions

How is this different from a correction note?

A note fixes one web page. Vimba links the claim in every copy, language and partner republication, and the same printed QR code shows the fix.

Who decides what is true?

The newsroom’s editors. Vimba signs and records what they approve. A complaint never changes a story’s public status on its own.

What stops spam or abuse?

Complaints are private and tied to an exact sentence. WhatsApp has rate limits and a monthly cap, and similar complaints are grouped.

Is readers’ data safe?

Phone numbers are encrypted and never logged. Each newsroom’s records are walled off by row-level security in the database.

Why would a newsroom pay?

A traceable record of every correction, complaints handled privately before they escalate, and every copy fixed in one step.

What is not built yet?

CMS plug-ins, a +263 number and .co.zw address, and voice notes on WhatsApp (voice works on the web today). The pilot adds them.
