THE PROBLEM

One story spreads across newsrooms.
Its correction can stop at the source.

A fictional example

A newsroom reports that floods closed

12 schools

The source corrects the figure to

2 schools

Smaller newsrooms republish it

still 12 schools

Radio stations translate it

still 12 schools

Readers forward graphics on WhatsApp

still 12 schools

Who tells everyone else?

Readers also need a private way to report errors and track the response.

(0:20–0:48 · 28 seconds) One example, all the way through. Say The Herald reports that floods closed 12 schools, then corrects it to 2. But smaller newsrooms have republished it, radio stations have translated it, and readers are forwarding the graphic on WhatsApp. They all still say 12. Who tells everyone else? And the reader who spots the error has no private way to report it and track the response.
